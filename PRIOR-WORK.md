# Related work

- **Akbari, Kumar, Mohar, and Pragada** ([arXiv:2609.27046, §1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27046#S1.SS4)) prove asymptotic versions of the main-eigenvalue switching conjecture and pose the general tree and connected regular graph question as Problem 1.6.
- **H. Dong** ([Paper 8](https://github.com/Horace-Maxwell/ai4math-results/blob/main/papers/switching-diam4/note.tex), [Lean statements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Horace-Maxwell/ai4math-results/main/lean/Research/Backfill/Paper8/Challenge.lean)) establishes the switching conjecture for all trees of diameter at most four and records the Krylov-rank characterization.
- **Hangxi Cha and Haiying Shan** ([arXiv:2607.13577v1, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.13577v1#S3)) introduce the diameter-five tree notation and uniform reflected families $T_r(a)$ and $T_r^p(a)$ used here, studying trees with three main eigenvalues for the unswitched all-ones vector.

The results in this repository concern the existence of a sign switching that makes every distinct adjacency eigenvalue main for diameter-five tree families, leaving the unrestricted diameter-five case open outside the proved families.
